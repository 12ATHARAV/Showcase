Our Story

Crafted with passion,
served with grace.

Lumière was born from a belief that dining is more than sustenance — it is theater. Chef Élodie Martin brings 15 years of Michelin experience to every plate, blending classical French technique with the freshest local produce.

Our 36-seat dining room offers an intimate setting where each course tells a story, and every detail — from the linen to the lighting — is designed to elevate your evening.
